Questions

Who is Namma Payroll for?

Growing companies that want one place for employees, attendance, payroll, compliance and payslips.

Does it handle PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS?

Yes. It calculates them in the payroll run and gives you registers and reports. Please have your tax advisor confirm the setup for your company.

Can employees see their own payslips?

Yes. Each employee has a home page with payslips, leave, attendance and documents.

Can I bring in my existing employee data?

Yes. You can import employees, attendance and leave from a file.

How do I see it before I decide?

Request a demo and our team will contact you to arrange it.
