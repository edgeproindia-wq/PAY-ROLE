Rules that are always enforced
  • One payment per payslip. The database refuses a second one.
  • A payment that is already Paid can never be put in a batch.
  • A payment that is in an open batch (waiting, approved, file generated or sent) cannot be put in another batch.
  • A bank UTR number can be recorded against only one payment.
